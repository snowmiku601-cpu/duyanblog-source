## Cross-cutting buyer risks (all from official pages)

- Direct thermal only across all five (no thermal-transfer model in this lineup; the F12/A30
  ribbon printers exist separately for that). Black-and-white output by default; colored
  effects come from colored/gradient thermal PAPER, not the printer (Phomemo sells
  colored/glitter/holographic thermal paper).
- Thermal fading: Phomemo's own paper catalog publishes differing lasting ratings — 2-year,
  10-year, 20-year lasting papers exist (paper-dependent, not printer-dependent). Cheap paper
  fades faster; this is the honest trade-off to state.
- Ongoing media costs (displayed store prices at check): 4×6 fan-fold labels $26.79; 53mm
  10-year sticker paper (3 rolls) $18.99; A4 thermal paper $25.89; D30 label rolls from the
  D30 accessory list. Media is proprietary-ish (per-model compatibility lists).
- Apps differ (official drivers-page mapping, phomemo.com/pages/drivers): Print Master = D30, M110 (mobile app; M110 also USB to computer); Labelife = PM241-BT (desktop USB driver route); Phomemo app = T02, M08F (mobile):
  PM241-BT USB from Windows/Mac + Bluetooth mobile; M08F Bluetooth + USB.
